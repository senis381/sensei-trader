📚 Reference: Indicators and RR

Sensei Trader uses several key indicators for market analysis. Here's what each means:

Core Indicators

1. VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price)

What it is: Volume-weighted average price. Shows "fair" asset price accounting for trading volumes.

How to read:

  • Price above VWAP (+0.5%) ✅ — bullish context, buyers control market
  • Price below VWAP (-0.8%) ⚠️ — bearish context, sellers dominate
  • Price at VWAP level (±0.2%) — equilibrium, uncertainty zone

Application: If planning a long, better enter when price above VWAP. For shorts — when below.

2. ADX (Average Directional Index)

What it is: Trend strength indicator (not direction!). Measured from 0 to 100.

How to read:

  • ADX < 20 — no trend, chop or weak movement
  • ADX 20-25 — trend starting but still weak
  • ADX 25-40 — moderate trend, tradeable
  • ADX > 40 — strong trend, high continuation probability

Application: In chop (ADX < 20) avoid trend strategies. At ADX > 25 — look for continuation.

3. Open Interest (OI)

What it is: Total number of open futures contracts. Shows whale interest.

How to read:

  • OI rising (+5%) 🟢 — new money entering, move confirmed
  • OI falling (-5%) 🔴 — participants closing positions, move weakening
  • OI stable (±2%) — uncertainty

Application:

  • Price rising + OI rising = ✅ healthy growth (new longs)
  • Price rising + OI falling = ⚠️ shorts closing (weak move)
  • Price falling + OI rising = ✅ healthy decline (new shorts)
  • Price falling + OI falling = ⚠️ longs closing (weak move)

4. RSI (Relative Strength Index)

What it is: Overbought/oversold indicator. Measured from 0 to 100.

How to read:

  • RSI > 70 — overbought, possible correction down
  • RSI < 30 — oversold, possible bounce up
  • RSI 30-70 — normal zone

Application: Use RSI as warning, not signal. RSI 75 doesn't mean "short now", but means "be careful with longs".

What is RR (Risk/Reward)?

RR (Risk/Reward) — ratio of potential profit to risk on one trade.

Formula:

RR = (TP - Entry) / (Entry - SL)

Example:

  • Entry: 45,000
  • SL: 44,500 (risk 500)
  • TP: 46,500 (profit 1,500)

RR = 1,500 / 500 = 1:3

How to read:

  • 1:3 — for every $1 risk you can make $3
  • 1:2 — minimum acceptable ratio
  • 1:1 — poor trade (risk = profit)
  • <1:1 — disaster (risk greater than profit)

Why Is RR Critically Important?

Even with 40% winrate you'll be profitable with proper RR:

10 trades, $10 risk each:
Winrate 40% (4 wins, 6 losses)
RR = 1:3

Losses: 6 × (-$10) = -$60
Wins: 4 × (+$30) = +$120

Total: +$60 profit at 40% winrate!

That's why Sensei Trader always checks RR before entry — it's the math of success.

Direction Priority: What Is It?

In analysis you often see "Priority: Continuation" or "Priority: Correction". What does it mean?

Priority — market bias based on indicators.

  • Continuation — current movement likely to continue (strong trend, OI rising)
  • Correction — pullback or reversal probable (RSI overbought, OI falling)
  • Neutral — no clear edge, chop or uncertainty

⚠️ Important: Priority is NOT entry signal! It's context for decision-making.

Example:

  • You want to long BTC
  • Bot shows "Priority: Correction (Short)"
  • Action: Wait for confirmation or look for another coin

Understanding these indicators turns you from a gambler into a conscious trader.

Trade Score 0–10: How Bot Analyzes Setup Quality

When you open or check a position, bot assigns a 0 to 10 score. This is not a profit prediction, but a quick check: how reasonable is the trade in terms of risk management.

Score based on:

1. Stop-loss Quality (SL)

  • 🟢 SL in profit (trailing) → score boost
  • ✅ SL adequate (3-7% from Entry) → score boost
  • ⚠️ SL too tight (<2%) → penalty (often stopped by noise)
  • ⚠️ SL too wide (>15%) → penalty (sign of poor setup)

2. Potential Risk/Reward (RR)

  • RR < 1 → major penalty (risk > reward)
  • RR 1–2 → minor penalty (mediocre)
  • RR 2–3 → boost (good math)
  • RR ≥ 3 → extra boost (excellent math)

3. Current Trade Phase

  • ≥ +1R → can move stop to breakeven
  • ≥ +2R → logical to take partial profit
  • ≤ −1R → weak development, consider reviewing

How to Read Score:

  • 8–10 — strong trade by math and risk
  • 6–7 — normal working trade
  • 4–5 — average, better not increase risk
  • 0–3 — weak, justified only with very clear scenario
⚠️ Important to understand:

Score 0–10 is not a signal and not result guarantee. It's a quick filter: how reasonable is the trade by risk management and plan.

Even a 9/10 trade can close in loss — market is unpredictable. But systematically trading 7+ setups instead of 3-4, you mathematically increase profitability chances.

Score Example:

Bot analyzes setup and assigns score:

  • Entry, SL, TP: Core trade parameters
  • Risk/Reward: Calculated ratio (e.g., 1:2.4)
  • Stop-loss assessment: Distance adequacy check
  • Final score: E.g., 6/10
  • Remarks: Specific warnings (e.g., "stop too wide — often sign of poor setup")

Based on this score you can decide: enter the trade, adjust parameters, or skip the setup.

This system helps filter impulsive entries and keep trade quality bar high.

🔑 How to Get Bybit API Keys (read-only)

1. Log into Bybit Account

Go to bybit.com and log into your account.

2. Open API Section

  • Hover over profile icon (top right)
  • Select API from dropdown
  • Or go directly: API Management

3. Create New Key

  • Click Create New Key
  • Select System-generated API Keys (recommended)

4. Configure Permissions (CRITICAL!)

⚠️ Important for security:

Select ONLY read-only permissions:

Check:

  • ✅ Read-Only (check the box)
  • ✅ Under it expand and check:
    • Wallet (balance reading)
    • Position (position reading)
    • Trade (trade history reading)

DO NOT check:

  • ❌ Contract Trade (trading)
  • ❌ Spot Trade (spot trading)
  • ❌ Wallet Transfer (transfers)
  • ❌ Withdrawal (fund withdrawal)

5. Save Keys

  • Click Submit
  • Copy API Key (long string of letters and numbers)
  • Copy API Secret (another long string)
  • ⚠️ Important: Secret shown only once! Save it immediately.

6. Paste Keys into Bot

  • Return to Telegram to Sensei Trader
  • Command /setkeys
  • Paste API Key
  • Paste API Secret
  • Done! ✅

Verification:

After setting up keys, check everything works:

  • Press button 💰 Balance
  • If you see balance — keys configured correctly!
  • If error — check that you selected Read-Only permissions
